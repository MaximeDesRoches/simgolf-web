 Tide Tables
 - Sample story for the Seaside Club theme pack

PARTNER, I hear you know every tide on this coast by heart.
 Forty years at sea will do that, MYNAME.
 Only the ones that ever cost me a ball.
 I know them better than I know this course.

Could you teach me to read the water? My putts keep breaking toward the ocean.
 Everything on this course breaks toward the ocean.
 Read the grass the way you'd read a swell.
 Lesson one: stop aiming for the ocean.

I tried what you said and sank a twenty footer!
 That's the finest thing I've seen since my old ship.
 Beginner's luck, but I'll take some of the credit.
 Next you'll be wanting lessons in knots.

Same time next week, Captain?
 I'll bring the tide charts if you bring the sunscreen.
 Wouldn't miss it for all the fish in the sea.
 Only if the weather holds.
